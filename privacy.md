# Privacy Policy — The Mechanic

**Last updated:** 2 November 2026  
**App:** The Mechanic, a free fan-made Elder Scrolls Online fight-guides app (trials, dungeons, arenas).  
**Owner:** Ivan Rodriguez.

This is a plain-language privacy policy for a small fan app. The Mechanic is **not affiliated with** Bethesda Softworks, Zenimax Online Studios, ZeniMax Media, or The Elder Scrolls® Online.

**This app is not for children under 13.** We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Data collection

The Mechanic **collects no data**. Fight guides and gear set tooltips are bundled in the app and run **on this device only**. **Nothing leaves the device.** There are **no accounts**, no cloud, and no messaging other players. This is not The Armory guild companion.

## What stays on this device

- **Favorites** — you can favorite a dungeon or trial for easier searching. That list stays in on-device storage.
- **Achievement progress** — achievements you mark as done stay in on-device storage.
- **Theme and accent color** — appearance preferences stay on this device.
- **Age acknowledgment** — the 13+ confirmation stays on this device.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes these. We cannot read them from anywhere else.

## What we do not collect

Nothing is sent to us. We do not collect email, names, location, contacts, health, payment cards, government IDs, device identifiers, or usage data. We do not sell or share data, and we do not use it to advertise to you.

## Analytics, crash reporting, ads, and tracking

**None.** The Mechanic has no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, and no tracking. It does not embed any analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking SDK.

## External link

The app makes no network requests on its own. A gear set tooltip has an optional **Open on ESO-Hub** link. Tapping it opens that set's page on eso-hub.com in your browser. That external website is governed by its own privacy policy.

## Apple

Apple may collect data under its own privacy policies, for example when you download the app from the App Store. That collection is done by Apple, not by The Mechanic.
